# LPF Raw (Vowel mode)

Only in the **Vowel** LPF mode: how much of the source's own highs comes through on top of the formants, unshaped.

- **0** (off): the clean Vowel filter - everything passes through the formant resonators.
- **25**: a buzzy character, with the original waveform audible in the highs.
- **50**: twice that.

Reach it with **CV pressed again** while Formant shift is selected (CV once more goes back), or as the fifth field of the
LPF menu. Saved with the sound or track. Affect Entire on a kit row sets it for every kit row.

Available when the LPF mode is Vowel.
